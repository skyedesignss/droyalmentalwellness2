For Referral Partners

Referring is simple and straightforward

We work with hospitals, schools, senior communities, and other providers to connect people with appropriate outpatient mental health and rehabilitation support.

Who We Partner With

Supporting the work you already do

We welcome referrals from organizations that support children, adults, and older adults in the community.

Hospitals & Healthcare

Discharge planning and outpatient follow-up for patients needing ongoing mental health support.

Schools & Education

Support for students and families when mental health concerns affect learning or daily functioning.

Senior Communities

Assisted living, independent living, and senior programs seeking outpatient mental health resources.

Case Managers & Providers

Social workers, case managers, and community providers coordinating care for their clients.

Our Services

Two clear paths of support

Both programs are delivered in a professional outpatient setting and tailored to individual goals.

Outpatient Mental Health (OMHC)

Therapy, psychiatric evaluation, and medication management for children, adolescents, and adults.

Psychiatric Rehabilitation (PRP)

Practical daily living skills, employment support, and community integration for adults.

How to Refer

A clear four-step process

We designed the referral process to be straightforward for busy partners. No lengthy intake is required from your side.

1

Download the form

Use the one-page printable referral form provided on this page.

2

Complete the details

Fill in the individual’s information, your contact details, and the reason for referral.

3

Send it to us

Return the completed form by email or fax. You may also call us to discuss the referral.

4

We follow up

Our team will review the referral and contact the individual or your office promptly.
